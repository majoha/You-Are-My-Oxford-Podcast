How to play

  1. A season is chosen at random. You and the computer each choose a secret joker from that season’s players.
  2. Take turns naming Oxford United players who made an appearance in any competition that season.
  3. You can challenge the computer’s last pick if you think that player did not appear that season. A correct challenge wins; an incorrect challenge loses.
  4. If either player names their opponent’s joker, they lose immediately.
